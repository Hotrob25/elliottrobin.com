  • Agent · Claude Code

    An agent runs my accounting end to end, business and personal: it generates my invoices, files my receipts, spots what’s deductible, keeps the books and prepares my tax returns all the way to filing.

    Above all, it tells me what to do: a remittance due on a given date, an instalment to plan for, a document to sign. It checks rates and deadlines online, so I no longer have dates to remember.

    It also optimizes my taxes: it looks across my businesses and my personal file, then flags missed deductions and planning opportunities. Its rule: optimize, never conceal.

    No software, no subscription: the books are files, and every balance is recalculated from the source documents.
